## Technical review

- One request and its context feed one frozen local-model forward pass.
- Shared hidden states feed parallel readouts and independent task heads. Heads do not feed one
  another. The three named tasks are examples, not a limit on the number of heads.
- Layer labels are schematic. Each head selects its own readout; the figure does not claim a
  universal layer or pooling method.
- Score arrows cross a separate host-policy boundary. Policy and compatible-head loading describe
  integration points in the architecture; the implementation record in the article states what
  the current runtime measures and what remains specified work.
- The lower panel shows independent, offline training. It is separate from runtime inference.

The illustration contains no experimental results. The latency figure remains generated by
[`scripts/render_one_forward_many_decisions.py`](../../../scripts/render_one_forward_many_decisions.py)
from the frozen experiment outputs. No dataset, probe weights, predictions or benchmark values were
changed to produce this illustration.

The prompt records the design request, not a promise of pixel-identical regeneration. The older
code-generated SVG/PNG diagrams remain separate technical artifacts; rerunning their renderer does
not overwrite this approved illustration.
